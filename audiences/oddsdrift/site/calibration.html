Calibration

For every market that resolved, we look at the YES price 24 hours before settlement (from our own snapshots) and compare it with the outcome. A perfectly calibrated venue resolves YES 70% of the time when priced at 70¢. Brier score: lower is better (0 = perfect, 0.25 = coin flip).
